About Us

A Family-Centered Vision, Every Day

Kirsis' Daycare was founded by Kirsis Tavarez with one simple belief: every child deserves a second home that feels safe, joyful, and full of possibilities. What started as a small family operation has grown into a beloved community institution.

Kirsis brings over 20 years of early childhood education experience and a deep passion for nurturing young minds. Our team shares her commitment to making every child feel seen, supported, and excited to learn.

Our Programs

What Makes Us Different

We go beyond basic childcare. Our initiatives are designed to nurture the whole child; bilingual enviroment, and personalized learning plans. We are a daycare that goes beyond babysitting.

📚

Early Literacy

Daily storytime, phonics, and letter play give children a head start on reading and communication skills.

🎨

Creative Arts

Painting, crafts, and imaginative play help children express themselves and develop fine motor skills.

🌿

Bilingual

We are a bilingual daycare that exposes children to English and spanish at an early age.

🧘

Social-Emotional Learning

We teach kids to name their feelings, resolve conflicts, and build empathy from an early age.

🍎

Nutritious Meals

Wholesome, balanced meals and snacks prepared fresh daily to support healthy growth.

🛡️

Safe Environment

Background-checked staff and secure indoor and outdoor facilities keep your child protected at all times.
